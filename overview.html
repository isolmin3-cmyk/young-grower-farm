Our Story

From cultivation to food development.

OUR STORY

Agricultural experience.
A wider purpose in food.

Established in 2008, Young Grower Farm has expanded from fruit cultivation and distribution into food processing, mushroom cultivation and research.

FROM THE FARM

Growing the foundation.

Our work began with cultivation. Blueberries and peaches remain central to our fruit business, supported by greenhouse and open-field production.

Building food processing capabilities allowed us to create products beyond the fresh harvest, including freeze-dried fruit chips and fruit powders.

Follow our history ↗
FROM MUSHROOMS TO FOOD MATERIALS

Research grounded in production.

Mushroom cultivation introduced a new direction. Brown-rice-based mycelial cultivation connects agricultural production with mushroom rice, extracts and food ingredient development.

Our purpose is to discover the value of agricultural ingredients and turn that knowledge into practical food products.

Growing with care, developing with purpose.

We have built our business through hands-on work in cultivation, processing and distribution. We will continue connecting that experience with research to create food products that earn the confidence of our customers and partners.
