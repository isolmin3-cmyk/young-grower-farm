Our Businesses

Bio Research & Development

Connecting agricultural experience with ingredient research.

BIO RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

Discovering ingredient value.
Developing food applications.

Our research builds on practical mushroom cultivation and brown-rice-based solid-state fermentation.

FERMENTATION TECHNOLOGY

Research that starts with cultivation.

Our team works with Phellinus, Cordyceps and lion’s mane mycelia. A brown-rice-based cultivation method is described in Korean Patent No. 10-2567995.

The work connects cultivation conditions and processing with the development of mushroom-derived food materials.

RESEARCH AREAS

Understanding what is in the ingredient.

Composition analysis and process development help guide our work on food materials.

Beta-glucan

A mushroom-derived compound studied within our ingredient development program.

Cordycepin

A focus of our Cordyceps cultivation and composition-analysis work.

Erinacine A

A focus of lion’s mane mycelial cultivation and standardization research.

FOOD DEVELOPMENT

Bringing research into food.

Our food development spans Bowigang mushroom rice, mushroom extracts and Mushrice Chips. The company also develops and sells foods including Albumin Phellinus Care and Cordycepin 20.

Current food products and research projects are introduced according to their development stage.
