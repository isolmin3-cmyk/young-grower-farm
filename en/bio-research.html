RESEARCH PLATFORM

Linking cultivation, analysis and development.

Our company research institute, established in 2021, builds on brown-rice-based mycelium culture. Compound data, including HPLC analysis, informs the study of ingredient characteristics and standardisation.

Grounded in documented analysis

Analysis reports describe the tested samples. Ingredient or product specifications and relevant documentation can be reviewed for a proposed collaboration.
